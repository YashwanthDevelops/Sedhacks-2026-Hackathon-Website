This system follows the user-supplied direction in `phases.md` and its 640 × 4,500 px reference. ## Type

- Self-host VT323 for body copy, navigation, event figures and the `₹10,000` prize amount.
- Self-host Pixelify Sans for display headings and the decorative footer wordmark.
- Keep full copy as live text. Do not use type artwork as a replacement for content.

## Composition and behaviour

- Follow the section order, copy, assets and layout constraints in `phases.md`.
- Use an approximately 70rem centred content column with 20px minimum mobile gutters.
- Keep the header fixed, the mobile menu compact, the timeline horizontal on wide screens and vertical on narrow screens.
- Use image-generated raster scenes for original atmospheric and chapter art; keep decorative images hidden from assistive technology.
- Avoid rounded card scaffolds, glass panels, soft shadows, repeating star rows and moving backgrounds.
- Keep the countdown, tabs, mobile menu, anchors and FAQ useful by keyboard and without JavaScript fallbacks.
- Keep the Register press cue and short mobile menu fade as the only motion. Respect reduced motion.
